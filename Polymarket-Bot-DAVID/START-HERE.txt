===============================================================================
  POLYMARKET QUANT BRIDGE
  Trading bot with a desktop dashboard - for Windows
===============================================================================

  TWO STEPS. That is all.

    1. Double-click   1-INSTALL-FIRST.bat      (once, takes a few minutes)
    2. Double-click   2-OPEN-DASHBOARD.vbs     (every time you want it)

  Then press "Start bot" in the window that opens.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BEFORE YOU START
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  You need Python. If you do not have it:

    - Go to  https://www.python.org/downloads/
    - Click the yellow "Download Python" button
    - Run the installer and TICK THE BOX that says
      "Add python.exe to PATH"   (bottom of the first screen)

  Keep these three folders together in one place. The bot needs all
  three and finds them by sitting next to them:

      polymarket-quant-bridge      the bot
      ploymarketbot                connects to Polymarket
      qc_lean_bridge               the Quant Bridge - the brain


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IT CANNOT SPEND YOUR MONEY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  It starts in SIMULATION, trading with pretend money. The dashboard says
  so in green at the top left. Real trading needs two settings changed
  AND a wallet key added - none of which happens by accident, and none of
  which is in this guide.

  Leave it running for a few hours. It learns from live market data as it
  happens, and that data cannot be downloaded afterwards. The first hour
  looks quiet. That is normal.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT YOU WILL SEE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Overview   the headline numbers, how it is doing, and whether anything
             is wrong - check this first
  Wallets    traders it found and ranked BY ITSELF from their results
  Unusual    odd behaviour it spotted, with the numbers behind it
  Positions  what it is holding right now
  Closed     finished trades, and what each made or lost
  Results    which KINDS of trade have actually made money
  Activity   every decision it has made, newest first

  See the screenshots folder for what it looks like.
